# Discussion Guide for Library Room Booking

## Study Overview

**Purpose.** Learn what adults settle first when organizing a library study room, what they check before committing and how they confirm plans with their group.

**Method.** Discovery interview, 30 minutes, semi structured. Recruit first time and repeat organizers who tried to reserve a room for two to six people in the past three months.

> Use the most recent example. Follow the participant's sequence. Record which questions you asked and which they skipped. This sample guide accompanies invented interviews.

## Screener Questions

**S1. Are you 18 or older?**
[ ] Yes, Qualify
[ ] No, Disqualify

**S2. When did you last try to organize a public library study room?**
[ ] In the last three months, Qualify
[ ] Earlier or never, Disqualify

**S3. What was your role in the most recent attempt?**
[ ] I organized or helped arrange it, Qualify
[ ] Someone else handled the arrangement, Disqualify

**S4. How many people was the room for?**
[ ] Two to six, Qualify
[ ] I do not remember, Borderline and clarify
[ ] One or more than six, Disqualify

**S5. How many times have you tried to reserve a library study room?**
[ ] Once, Qualify for first time group
[ ] More than once, Qualify for repeat group

**S6. Which channel did you try first?**
[ ] Web, phone, desk or another channel, Qualify and note for coverage

## Intro and Consent `[~3 min]`

> Before any real session, use approved participant materials. Confirm recording and the alternative, how notes or transcripts are processed, any AI use, who has access, retention and the actual withdrawal and deletion procedure. Obtain permission to record separately. Pause if essential details are unresolved.

> We are exploring how people arrange space at the library. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is there anything that would make this conversation easier or more comfortable for you?

## Warm Up `[~4 min]`

- **What kind of things bring you to the library?**
- **Tell me about a recent group study session.**
- **How did the group agree to meet?**

## Core Questions `[~16 min]`

### Choosing a room

**Q1. Walk me through your most recent attempt to arrange a study room.**
  - What was already decided?
  - What did you do first?

**Q2. How did you narrow down the options?**
  - What could change about the plan?
  - Tell me about an option you passed over, if any.

**Q3. What information did you look for before choosing?**
  - Where did you find it?
  - What did you do if anything was unclear?

### Confirming the reservation

**Q4. What happened after you decided on a room or stopped looking?**
  - What told you whether you had a reservation?
  - What did you check again later, if anything?

**Q5. How did the others learn where to go?**
  - What did you send or say?
  - What did you do if anything changed?

## Closing `[~5 min]`

- **Which moment took the most effort, if any?**
- **What part of that experience did we miss?**
- **Whose booking experience might be different from yours?**
- **What would make a conversation like this more comfortable?**

> Thank the participant. In a real session, explain the approved next steps and close the recording if applicable.
