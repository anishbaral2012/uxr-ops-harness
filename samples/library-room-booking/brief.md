# Library room booking brief

Synthetic exercise for the UXR Ops harness. The library, product team, sessions and participant statements are fictional.

A public library network is considering a mobile booking flow for small study rooms. Today, in the scenario, patrons can call a branch, ask at the desk or use a basic web form. The product lead needs to choose what the first prototype should emphasize: finding a slot across branches, checking room details, and sharing a reservation. The intended audience is adult patrons who organized or tried to organize a two to six person study session at a branch in the past three months.

The web form and staff process are scenario assumptions. No analytics, benchmarks or prior studies are supplied. The exercise covers research artifacts from planning through reporting. It involves no recruitment or data collection.
